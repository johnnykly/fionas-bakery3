## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS (+ small vanilla JS), real separate pages per route (/, /tartor, /smaker-priser, /om, /fragor, /bestall) so any host, including One.com, can serve it and each page is indexable. The current preview is a single-file artifact.

## Users
- Wedding couples in the Malmö/Lund area planning months ahead, wanting a cake that feels made for them.
- Families ordering birthday, kids' party, christening/baptism and babyshower cakes, often with a theme or character, on 1–4 weeks' notice.
- Companies ordering cupcakes, cakepops or macarons with their logo for events.
- Adults ordering playful celebration cakes (18th with Jägermeister, 60th with champagne, roulette, designer bags, book stacks, student caps).
Their job: see that Fiona can make *their* idea, understand price and lead time, and send an inquiry.

## Product Purpose
Fiona's Bakery is a one-person, made-to-order konditori in Arlöv run by Fiona. The site exists to turn visitors into custom-cake inquiries. Success = more well-specified inquiries (occasion, date, servings, flavours, idea) arriving by email/phone.

## Positioning
Fiona is both a konditor and a sugar-flower/sugarpaste artist: hand-made sugar flowers and hand-modelled figures on cakes baked to order, at a transparent per-slice price (60 kr/bit). Range spans romantic sugar-flower wedding tiers and bold themed/sculpted party cakes from the same hands.

## Operating Context
- Orders by inquiry → conversation (inspiration photos by email) → quote → Företagsswish payment → pickup.
- Pickup at Trossgatan 25, 232 36 Arlöv. Sells to everyone regardless of location, depending on availability. Delivery possible at an extra cost depending on distance.
- Lead time: decorated cakes 1–2 weeks; sugar-flower cakes preferably 1 month. Rush orders sometimes possible.
- Instagram @fionasbakery is the living portfolio; seasonal closures (e.g. summer weeks) announced there.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Cake: vanilla or chocolate sponge + two mousse fillings. Mousse I: blåbär, hallon, mango, passion, skogsbär, jordgubb, jordgubb/rabarber, pistage (+5 kr/bit). Mousse II: vit choklad, mjölkchoklad, mörk choklad, pannacotta, citron, nougat (+5 kr/bit). Other flavours on request.
- Prices: tårta 60 kr/bit (decoration quoted by complexity); bageritårtor 60 kr/bit (tiramisu, moussetårta, vit chokladcheesecake, prinsesstårta); bakelser I 25 kr/st min 12 (cakepops, macarons, mini cupcakes); bakelser II 45 kr/st min 4 (pannacotta/moussebakelse i glas, maränglollipop, mini popsicles, stora cupcakes); logo/tema/special +10 kr/st. Cupcakes: vanilj/choklad, marängsmörkräm.
- Godkänd för F-skatt. Payment Företagsswish.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "Fiona's Bakery"; Swedish language; red-rose motif and script wordmark from the original logo are the recognisable heritage (a redesigned mark is welcome).
- Must feel warm and personal (not cold/minimal), never wedding-only or frilly (kids' and playful cakes belong equally), never templated, and ordering must stay obvious.
- SEO priority: tårta Malmö, beställa tårta, bröllopstårta, konditori Arlöv, barntårta, doptårta, studenttårta, sockerblommor, cupcakes med logga.

## Product Principles
1. Show the cakes; Fiona's work is the proof.
2. Price and lead time are honest and visible, never hidden behind "contact us".
3. One path to an inquiry from every page.
4. Romance and play are equal citizens.
5. Local and personal: a real person in Arlöv, not a chain.
