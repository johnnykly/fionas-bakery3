Scope: whole marketing site for Fiona's Bakery (Hem, Tårtor, Smaker & priser, Om Fiona, Frågor, Beställ). Mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: couples, families, companies in Malmö/Lund see Fiona can make their idea, see price + lead time, send an inquiry. Proof: 38 real cake photos. Constraints: Swedish, SEO copy kept, no invented claims, lighter overall feel, warm not cold, not wedding-only, not templated, ordering obvious.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is tinted like Fiona's sugarpaste. Each occasion owns one paste colour, so romance and play share one palette. It refuses the category default of a pastel photo hero, a script logo and white sections.
OWN-WORLD: Light paste slabs (rose, pistachio, butter, sky, lilac) as whole-region grounds with softly rounded, rolled edges. Deep gel colours (berry red, ink plum) carry type and actions. Colour pots serve as swatches and chips. No boxed cards: hierarchy comes from overlapping slabs.
STORY: The visitor sees the range (romance and play), finds her occasion's colour, learns 60 kr/bit and the lead time, and fills in the order card where the estimate ticks as she types.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A rose slab covers the left 55% with a large headline in deep plum and a berry "Beställ tårta" pot-button. A pistachio slab overlaps on the right, holding a tall real cake photo (39). Along the bottom, a row of five colour pots names the occasions. Nav sits on top as paste-coloured tabs.
FORM: candidate 6 of 7 (food-colour gel palette), seed bea9f926. Signature interaction: choosing an occasion re-tints the page's paste colour; the order estimate ticks like a counter.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
